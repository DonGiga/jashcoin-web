How Liquidity Guardians Works

  • Participants voluntarily provide JASH + BNB liquidity to the eligible JASHCOIN liquidity pool.
  • The initial reward is issued only after the liquidity position has been verified on BNB Smart Chain.
  • The 30-day bonus becomes eligible only if the required liquidity position remains eligible for the full 30-day period.
  • If the eligible liquidity is withdrawn before the 30-day period ends, the participant simply becomes ineligible for the outstanding 30-day bonus.
  • There is no fine or penalty for withdrawing liquidity.
  • Rewards are calculated according to the verified eligible liquidity value and the applicable reward tier.
  • Each liquidity reward claim is subject to verification. Duplicate, manipulated or fraudulent claims may be rejected.
  • Season 1 Liquidity Guardian rewards are limited to an aggregate allocation of up to 1,000,000 JASH.
  • The campaign may close when the allocated Season 1 reward supply has been exhausted.
IMPORTANT RISK NOTICE

Providing liquidity involves cryptocurrency risk, including price volatility and impermanent loss. JASH rewards do not guarantee that a participant will make a profit or recover the value originally provided as liquidity.
