HOW IT WORKS

  • Provide JASH + BNB liquidity to the eligible JASHCOIN liquidity pool.
  • Submit your public BNB Smart Chain wallet address and the liquidity transaction hash through the Liquidity Guardians registration form.
  • The liquidity transaction will be checked on-chain before an initial reward is approved.
  • The initial reward depends on the verified eligible liquidity value.
  • The additional 30-day bonus becomes eligible only after the qualifying liquidity position has remained eligible for the full 30-day period.
  • If eligible liquidity is withdrawn before the 30-day period ends, the outstanding 30-day bonus is simply not awarded.
  • There is no fine or withdrawal penalty imposed by JASHCOIN for leaving the program early.
  • Duplicate, manipulated, misleading or fraudulent claims may be rejected.
  • Reward availability is subject to the remaining Season 1 Liquidity Guardians allocation.
  • Season 1 Liquidity Guardians rewards are limited to an aggregate allocation of up to 1,000,000 JASH.
IMPORTANT RISK NOTICE

Providing cryptocurrency liquidity involves risk, including price volatility and impermanent loss. The value of JASH, BNB and the liquidity position can increase or decrease. JASH rewards do not guarantee profit or recovery of the value originally provided as liquidity.
